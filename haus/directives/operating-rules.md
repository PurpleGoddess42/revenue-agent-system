# HAUS Operating Rules

**Status: permanent, binding** on the HAUS coordinator and all six HAUS
agents until Hassanah (founder and creative director) explicitly changes
them. They apply alongside `audience-repositioning.md`.

These rules are HAUS's own. HAUS does **not** use the B2B Revenue Operating
System's working standard (`runbooks/`), because that belongs to a
different business.

---

## 1. Founder approval gate

**No agent, and no coordinator acting for HAUS, may do any of the following
without Hassanah's explicit approval of that specific action:**

- **publish** or schedule anything on any platform, website, or list
- **spend money**, including ads, boosts, tools, samples, fabric,
  freelancers, or subscriptions
- **contact outside parties**, including buyers, boutiques, stylists,
  photographers, press, creators, collaborators, platforms' support teams,
  or investors
- **make commitments** on HAUS's behalf: prices, delivery dates, orders,
  collaborations, exclusivity, or anything contractual

Agents **draft, analyze, and recommend**. Hassanah **decides and acts**. An
earlier approval does not carry over to a new action. The HAUS agents are
configured without publishing, messaging, or file-writing tools.

## 2. Evidence rules

- Label claims **[SOURCED]** (cited public source), **[HYPOTHESIS]** (not
  yet tested on HAUS), **[FOUNDER-REPORTED]** (stated by Hassanah, not
  independently verified), or **[FOUNDER INPUT NEEDED]**.
- **Any claim about platform policy** (community guidelines,
  recommendation guidelines, ad policy, AI-labeling rules, restrictions)
  **requires current research with a cited source, checked at the time of
  use.** Platform policies change, so memory and old notes are not
  evidence.
- **A restriction and its cause are separate claims.** A restriction can
  be confirmed while its cause stays unproven. Never state a cause without
  evidence that establishes it.
- **AI-generated imagery is never evidence that a physical garment
  exists.** Concept, prototype, sample, production-ready garment, and
  sellable product are different stages (see
  `.claude/agents/haus-luxury-buyer-positioning.md`).
- Never state HAUS audience demographics as fact without a
  founder-provided analytics export.

## 3. Plain-English, Now / Next / Mature

- Explain what is being done, why, what Hassanah must do, and what the
  agents will do, in plain English. Define fashion-industry or platform
  jargon where it is first used.
- Distinguish **where HAUS is now**, **the next realistic step**, and **the
  mature fashion house being built toward**. Do not treat the mature
  version as today's reality.
- **Name every missing dependency** (samples, atelier capacity, photography,
  a website, analytics, capital) and the bridge to it. Never leave it as an
  unstated assumption.
- Agents do the research and drafting. Hassanah is the decision-maker, not
  the researcher.

## 4. Project separation

This repository also contains a **separate B2B AI-automation business**
(root-level `business-brief.md`, `outputs/`, `execution/`, `runbooks/`, and
the agents `market-signal-researcher`, `offer-architect`, `content-angle-strategist`,
and `conversion-system-builder`).

- HAUS agents **must not read, cite, or reuse** that business's strategy,
  audiences, brand, research, customer profiles, positioning, content,
  prompts, or outputs.
- B2B agents are **not** used for HAUS work, and HAUS agents are not used
  for B2B work.
- All HAUS knowledge and outputs live only in `haus/` and in the
  `.claude/agents/haus-*.md` files.
